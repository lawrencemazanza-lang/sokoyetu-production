Seller Policy

Standards for sellers listing, pricing, shipping and selling live on SokoYetu.

Last updated: 2026-05-14. This page is a launch-readiness draft for SokoYetu and should be reviewed before public launch.

1. Seller verification

Sellers may be required to provide name, phone number, business details, product category, delivery area and verification documents before receiving full selling privileges.

2. Product listing quality

Sellers must provide clear titles, accurate descriptions, correct prices, real stock levels and honest product images. Misleading listings may be removed.

3. Pricing and stock

Sellers should keep stock and prices updated. Products that are repeatedly unavailable after ordering may be hidden or restricted.

4. Order fulfilment

Sellers must process orders quickly, update order status and package items safely. The seller dashboard includes product, order and tracking tools to support this process.

5. Live selling

Live sellers must show products clearly, avoid false scarcity, answer buyers respectfully and only sell items they can fulfil.

6. Prohibited conduct

Sellers must not sell counterfeit products, unsafe products, stolen goods, prohibited goods, misleading products or products that violate customer trust and marketplace rules.
