Common questions
How do I track my order?

Open the order tracking page and enter your order ID together with the phone number used at checkout. This helps protect customer privacy.

What should I do if M-PESA payment fails?

Confirm that your M-PESA line has enough balance, then retry checkout. If the issue continues, submit a support request with your order number and phone number.

How do I request a refund?

Use the support request page and choose refund request. Include your order ID, checkout phone number and a clear explanation of the issue.

Can sellers publish products automatically?

During soft launch, seller and product approval should remain under admin review so that product details, images, availability and delivery readiness are checked.

Support note
For order help, always include your order number and the phone number used at checkout. For general support, contact support@mysokoyetu.co.ke.
