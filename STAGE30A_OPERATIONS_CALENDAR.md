# SokoYetu Mtaani Stage 30A: First 30 Days Operations Calendar

## Daily

- Check new orders.
- Check payment status.
- Check support queue.
- Check refund requests.
- Export orders and payments during soft launch.
- Review Render logs for errors.

## Twice weekly

- Review seller accounts.
- Review product images and descriptions.
- Check out-of-stock products.
- Confirm delivery process issues.

## Weekly

- Export products, sellers, support notes and users summary.
- Review launch risk register.
- Test checkout with sandbox.
- Confirm email forwarding status.
- Review admin tokens and access.

## Before switching to production M-PESA

- Confirm real PayBill/Till.
- Confirm production Daraja Consumer Key and Consumer Secret.
- Confirm production Passkey and shortcode.
- Confirm callback URL.
- Test a very small real payment.
- Confirm money settlement destination.
- Confirm refund handling process.
