Rules for using SokoYetu as a buyer, seller, admin or visitor.

Last updated: 2026-05-14. This page is a launch-readiness draft for SokoYetu and should be reviewed before public launch.

1. Marketplace role

SokoYetu provides a marketplace where buyers can discover products and sellers can list products. Some products may be listed directly by SokoYetu through approved sourcing or wholesale import workflows.

2. Accounts

Users must provide accurate account information and keep login details secure. Buyers, sellers and admins have different access rights. Admin tools must not be used by buyers or sellers unless authorised.

3. Products and pricing

Product prices, discounts, stock levels and descriptions should be accurate. SokoYetu may update, hide or remove products that are misleading, unsafe, unlawful, counterfeit, unavailable or incorrectly priced.

4. Orders and payments

Orders are confirmed based on product availability, payment status and seller acceptance where applicable. M-PESA payments should only be treated as complete after payment confirmation is received.

5. Live selling

Sellers using live shopping must show products honestly, avoid misleading claims, respond professionally and follow marketplace rules. SokoYetu may moderate or remove live sessions that break policy.

6. Restrictions

Users must not upload false content, attempt fraud, abuse the platform, sell prohibited goods, misuse customer data, copy another seller's content unlawfully or interfere with the website.

7. Changes

SokoYetu may update these terms as the platform grows. Continued use of the platform means the user accepts the updated terms.
