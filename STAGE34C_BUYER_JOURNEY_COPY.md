# Stage 34C: Buyer Journey Copy

Use this wording across public pages and customer support where needed.

## How ordering works

Browse products, review the product details, add the item to cart, confirm checkout details, pay through the provided M-PESA flow, keep your receipt, then track the order using your order ID and checkout phone number.

## Payment reminder

Before entering your M-PESA PIN, confirm that the prompt amount matches your order total. If you receive a receipt but the order appears pending, do not pay again immediately. Submit a support request with your receipt and order ID.

## Delivery reminder

Make sure your delivery address and phone number are correct. If the item is delayed, wrong, missing or damaged, submit a support request with the order ID, product name and evidence.

## Support evidence

Include:

```text
order ID
checkout phone number
product name
issue type
M-PESA receipt if available
amount paid
short description
```
